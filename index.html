06 / MY APPROACH

LLMs propose.
Tools test. Evidence decides.

Good QA isn't a pile of green checks. It's a repeatable process that separates assumptions from observations and confirmed results from guesses.

01 / UNDERSTAND

Model the behavior.

Clarify user intent, expected outcomes, state, and the conditions that matter.

02 / EXPLORE

Test the edges.

Exercise meaningful variations and focus on behavior users and teams rely on.

03 / VERIFY

Reproduce the evidence.

Repeat the scenario and keep enough detail for another person to confirm it.

04 / COMMUNICATE

Make the next step clear.

Explain impact, steps, expected versus actual behavior, and testing limits honestly.
