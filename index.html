04 / THE WAY I WORK

LLMs propose. Tools test.
Evidence decides.

Good QA isn't a pile of green checks. It's a repeatable process that separates assumptions from observations and findings from guesses.

01

Understand

Define expected behavior, the user journey, and the conditions that matter.

02

Exercise

Run focused checks and vary one meaningful condition at a time.

03

Verify

Repeat the scenario and preserve evidence so another person can confirm it.

04

Communicate

Report impact, steps, actual versus expected behavior, and limits honestly.
